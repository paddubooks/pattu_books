# Known issues and limits

- Independent educator review of all questions, illustrations and generated worked-example explanations remains outstanding. Some explanations are topic-based and less specific than a hand-authored explanation. Subtraction examples use source illustrations; animated crossing-out/highlighting is not implemented as a separate teaching sequence.
- Picture descriptions generally identify the prompt or choice rather than giving full nonvisual equivalents of every mathematical diagram. A complete screen-reader content audit remains necessary.
- Worked examples reuse selected existing question assets. A different question ID is selected from current practice; visually equivalent repeated subskills can still appear.
- Topic downloads need manual removal/redownload after content revisions. Browser quota/eviction and unsupported service workers can prevent offline availability. Offline downloading is intended for built preview/production, not Vite development.
- Catalogue search, filters and scroll positions are stored through the preference repository. Browser storage denial prevents durable restoration.
- Additional subject/grade routes require extending current routing/path helpers, although content types/repository boundaries support expansion.
- Source spreadsheets are preserved. Updated integration activity/coverage evidence is supplied as JSON reports, not rewritten spreadsheet workbooks.
- Books retains its original sample prices/catalogue and unset contact destinations. No changes to those sample business details are made.
- No public deployment, backend, accounts, cloud progress or exhaustive physical-device testing is included.

See handover.md and final machine reports for run-specific verification status.
